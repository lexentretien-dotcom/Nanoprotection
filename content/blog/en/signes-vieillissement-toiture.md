title: "What are the signs of an aging roof?"
description: "A simple guide to recognizing normal wear and telling what calls for action."
date: 2026-10-09
---

## Signs visible from the ground

A change in color, darker areas, lifted shingles or wavy lines are the first clues. Moss or algae on shaded slopes are common.

## Signs inside the house

Water stains on the ceiling, paint that blisters near exterior walls or moisture in the attic call for a quick check.

## When to act

The earlier you act, the more options remain. An inspection at the first doubt often costs less than waiting for a leak to appear.
