---
title: "Roof rejuvenation or replacement: how to choose?"
description: "The practical criteria for deciding between preserving an existing roof and replacing it completely."
date: 2026-10-09
---

Rejuvenation is for a roof whose shingles are still sound but worn on the surface. Replacement is needed when the structure of the covering is affected. The two are not competing: they answer different roof conditions.

## Questions to ask yourself

Are there active leaks? Are the shingles cracked or lifting? How long do you plan to stay in the house? What budget can you commit this year?

If the answer to the first or second question is yes, a treatment is generally not the right first step.

## What a treatment does not do

A surface treatment does not replace damaged flashing, rotten decking or destroyed shingles. If someone promises you otherwise, ask them to put it in writing.

## Ask for a second opinion

Whatever you choose, have the recommendation explained in writing. A good contractor accepts that you compare, and also tells you when replacement is the best decision.
