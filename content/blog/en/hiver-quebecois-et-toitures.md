## Freezing and thawing, first

Water seeps into small cracks, freezes, expands, then thaws. Repeated over several winters, this cycle enlarges defects.

## Ice at the roof edge

When heat from the house melts snow from underneath, the water refreezes at the roof edge and can back up under the shingles. Good insulation and ventilation reduce this risk.

## After winter

Spring is a good time to look at the roof's condition. Look for displaced shingles, granules in the gutters and moisture stains inside.
