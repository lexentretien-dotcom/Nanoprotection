## There is no single number

The lifespan of a shingle depends on its quality, the roof's orientation, attic ventilation, the installation and the climate. Two neighbouring houses can age very differently.

## The factors that matter most

The sun degrades the shingle's components over time. Water and frost seep into the smallest cracks. Poor ventilation lets heat build up under the roof.

In a climate like Quebec's, repeated freeze-thaw cycles are particularly demanding.

## What you can do

Having the roof inspected after harsh winters, keeping gutters clean, checking ventilation and repairing small defects quickly all extend a roof's useful life.
